Golden Basket Supermarket Sales Dashboard

# Executive Overview 
Developed an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard to monitor and analyze Golden Basket Supermarket's January–August sales performance, providing a centralized view of key sales and profitability metrics for data-driven decision-making.

# Business Problem
The project aimed to uncover sales trends, sales patterns, and regional sales performance to support impactful business decisions and improve overall sales performance.

# Skills & Tools

**Excel | Power Query | Power Pivot | DAX | Pivot Tables | Data Cleaning | Data Modeling | Data Visualization | Business Intelligence
**
# What I Did
- Loaded and cleaned raw sales data using Power Query.
- Removed duplicates and resolved data inconsistencies.
- Built relationships between tables using Power Pivot.
- Created DAX measures for key business KPIs.
- Used Pivot Tables to analyze sales trends and patterns.
- Designed an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard to present overall business performance.
- Transformed analytical findings into clear business insights and recommendations.

# Insights Uncovered
- Revenue reached ₦1.38B from 54,911 transactions, generating ₦439.6M gross profit.
- March recorded the highest monthly sales at ₦263.9M, while February recorded the lowest at ₦199.1M.
- Port Harcourt recorded the highest branch sales at approximately ₦282.1M.
- Snacks & Confectionery was the leading category with approximately ₦227.4M in sales.
- Peanuts 200G was the top-selling product, generating approximately ₦72.8M.
- Sales performance showed noticeable monthly variations, creating opportunities for deeper analysis of sales drivers and seasonality.

# Recommendations
- Maintain adequate inventory for high-performing products to minimize stock-outs.
- Replicate successful strategies from top-performing branches.
- Strengthen promotions, product bundling, and cross-selling within high-performing categories.
- Investigate low-performing categories to identify demand, pricing, assortment, or availability issues.
